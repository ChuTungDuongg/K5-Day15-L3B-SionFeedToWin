# Vintrip preview assets

Bundled from the approved mobile/design handoff. Font and photo attribution is recorded in `apps/mobile/assets/brand/README.md`.

- `regular.ttf` and `bold.ttf` preserve the original Be Vietnam Pro font bytes.
- `nunito-variable.woff2` is self-hosted [Nunito](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/nunito), converted from the official variable font. Its SIL Open Font License is in `Nunito-OFL.txt`.
- The wordmark SVGs use [Outfit](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/outfit) outlines: Medium 500 in the hero and SemiBold 600 in compact logos. Its SIL Open Font License is in `Outfit-OFL.txt`.
- `great-vibes.woff2` is self-hosted [Great Vibes](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/greatvibes), used for the hero slogan. Its SIL Open Font License is in `GreatVibes-OFL.txt`.
- Tripi PNGs and the Vintrip SVG use the approved local brand assets.
- Destination and hotel photographs illustrate the UI; they are not verified catalog media.
- `hero-river-landscape.jpg` is the original user-supplied photo, copied from `pexels-son-tung-tran-17375059-6531912 (1).jpg`. The hero wordmark keeps subtle travel-photo fills from the existing `hero-layered-v1/vintrip-logo-crystal.svg`, under translucent glass, specular edge lighting and an offset underside. `vintrip-wordmark-mask.svg` matches its outlines for the live CSS backdrop blur. The SVG remains readable without backdrop-filter support.
- `home-hoi-an-lanterns.jpg` is [Khoi Tran’s photograph of Hội An](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-tree-with-a-bunch-of-colorful-paper-lanterns-hanging-from-its-branches-NPIJb6T4tO4), available under the Unsplash License.
- `flight-sky.jpg` is [willy wo’s photograph of an airliner in the clouds](https://unsplash.com/photos/white-and-blue-airplane-under-white-clouds-during-daytime-pXnMG0FfSwA), available under the Unsplash License.
- `hotel-resort-4k.webp` is [Asad Photo Maldives’s resort photograph](https://www.pexels.com/photo/luxurious-overwater-bungalow-with-infinity-pool-at-sunset-30037427/), available under the Pexels License.
- `home-stays-ninh-binh.jpg` is [Florian Delée’s photograph of Ninh Bình](https://unsplash.com/photos/lush-green-karst-mountains-with-a-small-village-below-MshJQhMq94w), available under the Unsplash License.
